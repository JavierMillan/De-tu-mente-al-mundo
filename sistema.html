What if I already have a website?

We review your existing site and what you need before agreeing on scope. This offer covers a new page. Changes to an existing website or connections to other systems require assessment and may need a separate quote.

When do the 72 hours start?

Once we’ve agreed on scope and have the required materials. We confirm the delivery date before starting. More complex workflows get their own quote and timeline.

Are there monthly or extra costs?

We don’t charge a required monthly fee. Domain, hosting, third-party services or payment processing may have costs; we disclose them before you commit. Later changes and advanced integrations are quoted separately.

Does this bring in leads or guarantee sales?

It works with the inquiries you already receive. It collects details and applies agreed criteria; it doesn’t guarantee sales or a percentage of time saved. Appointments, availability and quotes are confirmed through your business process.

Can I write this off? How do you invoice from Mexico?

Yes. You get a USD invoice itemizing the work, and if your accountant asks for it, we sign a W-8BEN confirming we are a foreign vendor. Because the work happens outside the US, no 1099 is required. Keep the invoice and the contract and your accountant records it like any other business expense. We are not accountants, so confirm with yours.

Can we work in Spanish?

Yes. We’re based in Mexico and work in English and Spanish. This offer is for US businesses; pricing and scope are the same in either language.
